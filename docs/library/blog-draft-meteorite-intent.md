Real meteorites sell at auction for up to $5.3 million — a 24.67 kg piece of Mars set the record at Sotheby's New York in July 2025. If you are wondering where to buy a real meteorite this year, the short answer is: two auction houses and a handful of licensed dealers. The longer answer — including the one meteorite you can actually sit on — is below.

## The Auction Houses: Sotheby's and Christie's

Sotheby's held its first auction dedicated entirely to meteorites as part of its Geek Week series, with entries starting around $500. Christie's runs periodic "Rare Meteorites" sales — one featured the third-largest piece of Mars on Earth among 66 lots, with estimates from $400 to $800,000.

Auctions are where the record prices live. Lunar and Martian specimens trade above $1,000 per gram, because only around 400 Martian meteorites are known to exist. For a first-time buyer, auction premiums and bidding competition make small stones the realistic entry point.

## What a Real Meteorite Actually Costs

Dealers sell authenticated specimens year-round, not just at auction. The pricing logic is simple: the rarer the origin, the higher the per-gram price.

- Unclassified stone meteorites: roughly the price of a nice dinner per gram
- Iron meteorites (the sculptural ones, etched with crystalline Widmanstätten patterns): hundreds of dollars per gram
- Lunar or Martian material: $1,000+ per gram — more than gold by an order of magnitude

The collector market has grown fast enough that both major auction houses now treat it as a standalone category. When a single rock sells for more than a penthouse, the market has spoken.

## Why Most Meteorites End Up in a Cabinet

Here is the strange part about owning a meteorite: after the thrill of acquisition, it becomes an object you look at. A stone in a display case. The most common regret among first-time collectors is not the price — it is that the piece has no daily life in the room.

A meteorite is, literally, the oldest thing you will ever touch. It deserves better than a drawer.
