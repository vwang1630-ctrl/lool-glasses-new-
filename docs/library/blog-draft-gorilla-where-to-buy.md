If you are searching "where can I buy a gorilla sofa," here is the honest answer up front: real, sculpted gorilla sofas come from a small number of ateliers that build them to order; marketplaces like Amazon, AliExpress and Temu sell plush and inflatable versions; and a few galleries resell designer pieces at five figures. What follows is each option — and the three checks that separate a decade of furniture from a disappointment.

## Option 1: Marketplaces (Plush & Inflatable)

Amazon, AliExpress and Temu list plush gorilla couches and inflatable "King Kong" loungers, mostly between $50 and $300. They photograph well. In person they are what the price says: printed fabric over light foam, or air. For a themed party that lasts one night, they are the right tool. As furniture, they are not in the conversation.

## Option 2: Designer Marketplaces & Galleries

Design platforms and galleries occasionally list animal-form statement seating from established designers, typically $5,000 to $32,000. You are paying for the designer's name and gallery provenance — legitimate, if that is the collection you are building.

## Option 3: Atelier-Built Originals (Made to Order)

A small group of studios sculpts animal sofas as original works — clay form, welded steel skeleton, shaped foam, hand-sewn skin. This is where the [Kong Tender Titan](https://fuzzsofa.com/en/kong-tender-titan-monumental-sculpture-sofa.html) sits: a 200 × 160 × 152 cm gorilla rated to 200 kg under structural QC protocol FUZZ-LAB-2024-01, with a serialised inspection report in every crate. $9,900, built to order in 1–3 days, delivered door-to-door in 7–14.

## Three Checks Before You Pay Anyone

1. **Load rating with a number.** "Sturdy" is not a spec. Ask for the rated capacity and the test that proves it.
2. **Frame material.** A gorilla sofa curves everywhere; particle board and soft wood fail exactly where the arms curve. You want welded steel.
3. **What happens if it doesn't fit.** A serious maker verifies your room before production. If nobody asks about your doorways, that tells you everything.

The same checks apply to the rest of the animal kingdom, by the way — the [Noctua Owl Armchair](https://fuzzsofa.com/en/products/noctua-owl-armchair.html) passes all three for owl people.
