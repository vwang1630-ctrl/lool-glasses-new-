# 高客单成交对话 Playbook V1.0（骨架版）

> 定位：FDE 前线技能·成交对话的**可预备骨架**（2026-10-06 立）。异议应答=框架 T3，
> 每一条真实顾客对话发生后回填实战案例升级 T1。配合：customer-language-corpus（语料）、
> outreach-round1（抗拒点 4 条已锁）、口径（200kg/1-3+7-14/DDP/无折扣）。

---

## 一、高客单心理底牌（先记这个再记话术）

1. **买家买的是身份表达，不是家具**——"镇宅/收藏/我是那种人"。价格异议的真相往往是"我还不确定这值得我成为那种人"
2. **信任 > 速度**：$2,600-9,900 的决策周期 1-4 周。追得越紧死得越快；**教育式回复**比催单式回复转化高
3. **每个异议背后是恐惧**：贵=怕不值；运输=怕损坏；交期=怕落空；手工=怕争议。**回应恐惧，不要回应字面**
4. **小承诺升级**：先要房间照片→出预览图→报价——每一步小"是"，累积成大"是"

## 二、对话六阶段

```
触达 → 破冰(点对方具体上下文) → 需求发现(问>答) → 价值呈现
→ 异议处理(恐惧→证据) → 小承诺(照片确认/房间预览) → 报价成交
```

### 需求发现四问（问完再谈产品）
1. 这个放你家里哪个位置？
2. 你现在那件家具哪点让你想换？
3. 家里/空间有门到门的照片吗？（自然过渡到验证流程）
4. 你是给自己的，还是送人/商业空间？（影响口径：自用讲治愈，商业讲独家）

## 三、异议矩阵（10 条 × 应答，英文可直接用）

### 1. "Too expensive" / "$9,900 for a sofa?"
```
Fair reaction — most sofas cost less. Here's the difference: printed fabric
on light foam starts at $899. What we build is a welded steel skeleton rated
to 200 kg, cold-cure foam that never collapses, and a serialised inspection
report with your own unit. It's not a pricier sofa — it's a different thing.
```
**要点**：不贬低便宜货，给阶梯（$899 印花/$9,900 工程/$32,000 画廊）；把对比从"沙发 vs 沙发"换成"印花 vs 工程品"

### 2. "Is this real / is it AI?"
```
Real — and here's how you verify: every crate carries a serialised QC
report, we send real workshop photos of your own unit before shipping,
and the foam mold photos are on our profile. Renders don't have mold stages.
```
**要点**：给可核验证据链（QC 序列号/工坊实拍/模具阶段照），不空喊"real"

### 3. "Will it get damaged in shipping?"
```
It ships in a reinforced ISPM-15 export crate, foam-wrapped, insured.
White-glove crew places it in your room and removes all crate material.
If anything's damaged in transit, it's on us — documented and replaced.
```
**要点**：ISPM-15/白手套/保险/责任归属四件套一次讲完

### 4. "How long does it take?"
```
Built to order in 1–3 days after you approve the fit photos, delivered
door-to-door in 7–14. The photo check comes first — we don't build
blind.
```
**要点**：口径只此一种（1-3+7-14）；"先确认再生产"=反行业惯例的信任点

### 5. "Will it fit through my door?"
```
Send a photo of your space and entrances — we verify before production.
If it doesn't fit, we tell you before we build, not after it arrives.
```

### 6. "Is it pure handmade?"
```
Hand-finished details over an engineered frame. The sculpture is shaped
by hand; the structure is engineered so it holds 200 kg. Craft where it
matters, engineering where it counts.
```
**要点**：口径锁定（不说纯手工也不否认）；"craft + engineering"双框架

### 7. "Any discount?"
```
The price is the price — what you get is a QC report with your own serial
number and one workshop serving one partner per city. No discounts; that's
how the quality stays the quality.
```
**要点**：无折扣=品质纪律的表述，不是拒绝的话术

### 8. "How do I know you're legit / not a scam?"
```
Don't take my word: workshop photos and mold-stage records are public,
every unit ships with a serialised inspection report, and payment goes
through protected channels (PayPal/wire with documentation). Verify
before you pay — we insist on it.
```
**要点**：主动邀请验证（"we insist on it"）=最强信任话术

### 9. "What's the frame made of / will it collapse?"
```
2.5 mm Q235 welded steel tube under 45 kg/m³ cold-cure foam. Rated 200 kg,
load-tested against EN 1728. Sit on the lap, pile friends on — computed
for it.
```
**要点**：材质数字连报（钢号+泡沫密度+额定+标准）=专业感的来源

### 10. "Why so serious / it's just a couch"
```
It's the piece everyone talks about when they walk in. Some people buy
a sofa to sit; you're buying the thing your room is arranged around.
Different job, different build.
```
**要点**：身份表达框架收尾（镇宅物，不是坐具）

## 四、节奏规则

1. 当日回复，24h 必答，不过第二天中午（SLA 已锁）
2. 每条回复 ≤120 词为主，一问一答不倾倒
3. 连续 2 轮无回复 → 停，等对方；3 天后可发一条轻价值（新过程照）
4. **报价只在需求发现后**——上来就报价=把自己变客服
5. 成交前必走：房间照片确认（fit check）——既是服务也是承诺升级
