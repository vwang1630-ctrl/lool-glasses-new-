# 评论区 SOP — loollook.com

> 触发条件：任意社媒帖子收到第一条真实用户评论时启用

## if/then 回复规则

| 用户评论包含               | 判断     | 回复话术                                                                                                 | 禁止                         |
| -------------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| virtual try on / 虚拟试戴  | 功能咨询 | "Yes — our try-on uses your real camera. See yourself wearing them before you buy. Link in bio."         | 禁挂链接在 caption           |
| PD / pupil distance / 瞳距 | 功能咨询 | "You can measure PD at home with your phone — takes 30 seconds. No doctor visit needed."                 | 禁说'presbyopia'             |
| progressive / 渐进         | 产品咨询 | "Most people adapt in 3 weeks — and we guarantee it. One pair for near, far, and everything in between." | 禁提 adaptation failure rate |
| price / how much / 多少钱  | 价格询问 | "Frames from $6, lenses from $15. Progressive lenses available with Essilor/ZEISS. Link in bio."         | 禁在评论区报具体折扣         |
| where to buy / 哪里买      | 购买意向 | "loollook.com — free shipping worldwide. Or DM us for help."                                             | 禁贴裸链接在评论区           |
| face shape / which frame   | 选框咨询 | "Round, square, oval, heart — we have a guide for every face shape. Link in bio."                        | 禁用 senior/elderly          |
| old looking / 显老         | 负面顾虑 | "Ageless design — made for people who want to look great, not their age."                                | 禁说'we know you're not old' |
| scam / fake / 假的         | 质疑     | "Real camera try-on. Essilor and ZEISS lenses. 30-day returns. What would convince you?"                 | 禁情绪回复/禁拉扯            |

## 通用纪律

- 首次回复必须有一个具体动作（Link in bio / DM us / Try it yourself）
- 不用 "Thank you!" 空回复——每条回复都推进一步
- 负面评论不删不辩——用事实一句话回应，不再跟
- 全部回复走品牌号，禁伪装买家
- **绝对禁用词**：senior, elderly, old, aging, anti-aging, presbyopia, factory, handmade

## DM 转化流程

```
用户评论或 DM → 品牌号回复（引导 DM / Bio link）→ 站内虚拟试戴 / 客服跟进
```

- 社媒内容 CTA 统一为 "Link in bio" 或 "DM us"
- **IG Caption 内绝不挂 https:// 链接**（限流）
- 链接走 Bio 页
