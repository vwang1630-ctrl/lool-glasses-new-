# 02 · 给货代/报关行的委托询价函（发 3 家比价）

> 用法：把下面英文部分整段发给货代业务员（微信/邮件均可），中文部分是你自己的对照说明。
> 候选：顺丰国际、DHL Global Forwarding（注意是 GF 货代部门，不是 DHL Express）、
> 深圳做美国双清的 DDP 专线公司。**一次发 3 家**，同口径比价。

---

## 英文函（直接发）

Subject: DDP LCL Sea Freight Shanghai → US, Importer of Record = Our Chinese Company (EIN in application)

Hi [Name],

We are FUZZ [公司英文全名], a China-based designer furniture studio. We ship made-to-order
sculptural sofas from Shanghai to US consumers, door-to-door DDP, roughly 4–10 shipments
per month to start.

Our company will act as the **Importer of Record** for US customs. We are obtaining our EIN
from the IRS (application in progress). We need your US brokerage side to handle everything
under our identity:

1. File **CBP Form 5106** to register us as importer (company legal name and address below);
2. Provide **Power of Attorney** template for us to sign;
3. Arrange **single transaction bonds** per entry (our goods are furniture, no AD/CVD — please confirm);
4. File **ISF (10+2)** for ocean shipments;
5. Advance duty payments and bill us per entry (no US bank account on our side — please confirm
   this works and show the duty statement for each entry);
6. Confirm the **current total duty rate** for upholstered furniture, **HS 9401.61.00**,
   country of origin **China**, so we can double-check against your quote.

Typical cargo (LCL, wooden crates, one shipment = 1–2 crates):

- Crate A: 125 × 105 × 85 cm, 35 kg (armchair)
- Crate B: 125 × 135 × 75 cm, 38 kg (armchair)
- Crate C: 115 × 115 × 85 cm, 25 kg (accent chair)
- Crate D: 165 × 205 × 157 cm, 80 kg (1–2-seat sofa, occasional)

Destination: US residential addresses (liftgate + curbside; white-glove last mile quoted separately).

Please quote per shipment, DDP door-to-door, itemized:
**ocean freight + ISF/entry filing + bond + duty (at the confirmed rate) + delivery**,
and state your transit time (port-to-door) and how duties are invoiced to us.

Company details for 5106:
- Legal name: [与 EIN 申请完全一致的英文名]
- Address: [中国地址，拼音/英文]
- Responsible party: [法人护照拼音]
- EIN: [拿到后填] / Status: applied, EIN expected within days

Thank you.

---

## 中文对照（自己看，别发）

要点就六个：替我们交 5106、给 POA 模板、代买单票 bond、报 ISF、垫税按票开税单、
报当天 9401.61.00 中国原产的真实税率。让他们**分项报价**（海运/报关/bond/税/派送），
别接受一个含糊的总包价——分项才比得出谁在赚你哪一段钱。

**比价时盯三点：**
1. 税率单是否敢给你看（不敢看的 = 想在税上做手脚的，pass）；
2. bond + 录入费是不是在 $150–300/票的合理区间（收 $500+ 的在宰你）；
3. 海运时效口头的 25–35 天能不能写进报价单。

**红线：发票货值必须真实。** 让货代按真实货值申报；主动劝你低报/拆单的货代
（省税是他们常打的"卖点"）直接换掉——被海关盯上，罚款扣货上黑名单，网站 DDP 承诺整个塌。
